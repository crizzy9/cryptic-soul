# Organization marks

Verified against the organizations' public websites on 2026-11-17 for identification
of employment and education in the CV. These marks belong to their respective
owners; their inclusion does not imply endorsement or a general reuse license.
Files are served locally rather than through a logo lookup/tracking service.
All published marks use PNG with tight artwork bounds and preserved proportions.

- MosaicApp / Chello: `mosaic.png` (256 × 255), the official MosaicApp diamond icon.
  Source page: https://www.mosaicapp.com/
  Original asset: https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/60b7d9f08337276663c17190/6a61fb2c9e83c822dbcf67a2_mosaic-icon.webp
  Identity cross-check: https://www.mosaicapp.com/meet-chello
  Processing: removed the original transparent border, resized to 256px wide,
  and converted to optimized PNG. Supersedes `mosaic.webp`.
- Intuit: `intuit.png` (180 × 180), the official public website icon.
  Source page: https://www.intuit.com/
  Original asset: https://www.intuit.com/oidam/intuit/ic/en_us/images/01-g/Intuit_FavIcon_180x180.png
  Processing: lossless PNG optimization; the original artwork already reaches
  its transparent canvas bounds. The blue circle is part of the official mark.
- Northeastern University: `northeastern.png` (118 × 92), its official red N.
  Source page: https://www.northeastern.edu/
  Brand reference: https://brand.northeastern.edu/logos/monogram/
  Original asset: https://brand.northeastern.edu/global/assets/favicon/android-chrome-144x144.png?v=2
  Processing: recovered transparency from the original white matte using the
  source artwork's exact #D41B2C red, preserving antialiased edge coverage,
  then cropped to the N's bounds and optimized as PNG. No shape distortion.
- OutreachCircle: `outreachcircle.png` (192 × 192), its official multicolor circle mark.
  Source page: https://outreachcircle.com/
  Original asset: https://blog.outreachcircle.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/07/cropped-Assets_web-512-color-01-192x192.png
  Current identity cross-check: https://client.outreachcircle.com/assets/favicon.ico?12345
  Official rename announcement: https://blog.outreachcircle.com/2019/09/04/votercircle-is-now-outreachcircle-why-we-decided-to-start-over-from-scratch/
  Processing: lossless PNG optimization; the source already has tight transparent
  bounds. The official founder announcement confirms the VoterCircle rename;
  the current client site still uses this mark. This is unrelated to Outreach.io.

- University of Mumbai: `mumbai-university.png` (191 × 192), its shield-and-tower
  crest, including the open book, 1857 date and motto ribbon.
  University identity reference: https://mu.ac.in/
  Official brochure cross-check: https://mu.ac.in/old/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/New-Mumbai-University-Admission-EBrochure-2025-FIN.pdf
  Accessible vector source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:University_of_Mumbai_coat_of_arms.svg
  Original asset: https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/en/7/7a/University_of_Mumbai_coat_of_arms.svg
  Processing: rasterized the monochrome vector, trimmed the transparent border,
  and resized proportionally to a 192px maximum dimension. The CV inverts this
  monochrome mark in its dark theme for legibility; no white tile is added.
  The official homepage timed out during retrieval. The university's indexed
  official brochure confirms the same crest; the downloaded file is Wikimedia's
  university-identification logo, not a logo of an affiliated college. Copyright
  and trademark remain with the university; no general reuse license is claimed.
